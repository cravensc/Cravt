You Had Me
at Hello

Walking into Motoworks, I had no expectations. I wasn't shopping for myself and knew very little about bikes. I didn't expect to find a place I didn't want to leave.

I immediately felt like a friend. Your staff lit up when we walked in. They were excited when I asked questions and gave me explanations I could actually follow as a complete novice.

Motoworks is an inspiration for how to welcome people, teach them something real, and run a business that makes people leave smiling. If there's a way to make it work, I would love to learn how you do it and be a part of it.
