From My Grandma's Farm

The Corkins History Is Inspiring

As a kid, having wide open land to explore was a privilege I appreciate more and more as I see the place that I grew up in, Northwest Arkansas, turn into a fully developed corporate metropolis. My grandma's small farm in Gravette was where I went fishing with my cousin Joshie and created "hideouts" in trees with my sister and rode horse with my Aunt Jill.

The accessibility to wild land was essential to the memories of my childhood I cherish most. I've experienced the magic of being in the country, forced to put down your phone and figure out the most interesting thing to do with some rocks, and I admire how you are giving people a space to do that. You've struck a chord in my sentimental lil heart, and I want to help you bring friends and families together to explore nature and, without knowing it, create the fondest memories of their life.

Wrangling 30+ Students.

Who knew how hard it would be to get 30+ students to complete assignments on time? I figured it out. I realized students need exhaustive, clear assignment descriptions, MULTIPLE in-person and digital reminders, and even individual nudges. I have learned how to wrangle a bunch of unruly undergrads to be on the same page, so I am battle tested to handle logistics coordination with care. Humble brag: I am great at learning names, which is extremely helpful when developing an empathetic and respectful relationship with students.

Grading, Disputes, and Keeping It Professional.

The professors I worked with were generally busy people, so they trusted me with grading individual assignments and calculating final grades. Students have a lot of feelings about their grades. Inevitably, I was the one they came to, in person and over email, to talk through grading decisions. I learned how to hold a fair line with empathy, and how to have those conversations without making anyone feel dismissed.

Speaker Logistics, Down to the Water Bottle.

Many of the classes I helped with brought in guest speakers. These speakers need to know the location, where to park, how to park for free, how to get to the classroom, when to arrive, and what to talk about. The day of a visit, I am reachable by phone for any need, water bottle in hand, gift ready. I made sure the speaker experience was so good they would want to come back, and many did.

A Three-Month Gig
That Turned Into Two Years.

This job was only supposed to last for 3 months. Techstars Oakland was asking students for short-term help with the operations of their program. I literally passed a vibe check and became a part of the team. Hours before my employment was supposed to end, I was asked by the Managing Director, Neal Sales-Griffin, and Accelerator Lead, Lilliana Robinson, if I would stay on and help with the next program, and the next, and the next. The Techstars programs we ran were some of the best funded, largest, and most successful programs in Techstars history.

Coordinating 50+ Zoom Sessions.

Each program, Techstars brings in successful business mentors to give feedback to every company, which means coordinating dozens of back-to-back Zoom meetings across 12-24 startups. My role was to navigate founders in and out of those sessions: managing waiting rooms, timing transitions, sending follow-up emails to the stragglers. After each round, I manually recorded and cleaned up all the mentor feedback so it could be delivered in a usable format. The work was tedious, and exactly the kind of thing that has to be done right.

Designing an Experience Founders Remember.

The programs I was a part of were hybrid, 10-week programs with 3 in-person weeks. Our ability to deliver a great experience for founders during those weeks was essential. This meant office setup, which I took great pride in designing and executing (interior design is a bit of a passion). It also meant catering coordination: given a budget and told to figure it out. Under my care, we stuck to budget, always had plenty of food, and no one went to the hospital for an allergic reaction. During workshops, I handled AV, Zoom management, setup, and teardown.

The Largest Demo Day in Techstars History. I Helped Run It.

Demo Day is the final event where founders present their businesses to investors and the public, and for the largest Demo Day in Techstars history, there were over 600 people in the room. At any moment I might be making a last-minute grocery run and assembling a charcuterie board for eBay executives, or backstage running lines with a founder so they feel confident when they walk out. I have been in the room when it all comes together, and I know what it takes to get it there.
